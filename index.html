About the Cantata

Many voices.
One harmony.

The Harmony Academy Cantata Celebration brings our school community together through the power of music. Learners, teachers and special guests will share songs and performances that celebrate unity, hope and the creativity within us all.

Our theme, “A Symphony of Voices”, reminds us that every person has something valuable to contribute. From powerful choir arrangements to thoughtful solo performances, the afternoon promises inspiration, joy and school spirit.
